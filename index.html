About

25+ years of making offices work.

David got into technology in the early 1990s through a high school NJROTC class and a Macintosh. That turned into 25+ years of enterprise IT at companies including Apple, Facebook, where he helped build IT operations, and Twitter, followed by startups and growing companies where he built teams and tightened up systems.

He founded BACS Tech to give organizations a stronger IT foundation. Bored Software comes from the same place: the meeting-room signs, lobby sign-ins and package logs that every office IT team ends up owning, done simply and for 99¢.

Good IT is less about tools and more about people, process and strategy.
